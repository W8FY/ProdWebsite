## 🛰️ Frequency Info

- **Frequency:** 146.850 MHz  
- **Offset:** -0.600 MHz   
- **PL Tone:** 107.2 Hz
- **Mode:** FM
- **EchoLink:** W8FY-R
- **EchoLink Node:** 315705

## 📡 Usage

This is the primary VHF repeater operated by **W8FY**. It is used for:

- Weekly Saturday Morning Net at **10:30 AM**
- Talk-in for events like Hamfest and Field Day
- Skywarn operations during severe weather

## 🌐 EchoLink

The repeater is available through **EchoLink** using the station callsign **W8FY-R**, node **315705**.

To connect remotely, open the EchoLink app, search for **W8FY-R** or node **315705**, and select the station. The `-R` suffix identifies it as a repeater gateway.

[Learn more or download EchoLink](https://www.echolink.org/)

## 🌪️ Skywarn & Weather Nets

This repeater is the official Skywarn coordination frequency for Van Wert County. Please monitor during storms and follow Net Control instructions.
