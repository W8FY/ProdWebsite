    <div>
      <h2 id="what-is-radio">What is amateur radio?</h2>
      <p>Amateur Radio, also known as Ham Radio, is a licensed radio service for people who enjoy communication, technology, experimentation, and public service. Ham radio operators can communicate locally, across the country, and around the world using voice, Morse code, digital modes, repeaters, and even amateur radio satellites.</p>
      <p>Amateur Radio is also an important public-service resource. During severe weather, emergencies, and other situations when normal communication systems may be unavailable or overloaded, amateur radio operators can help provide reliable communications.</p>

        <li>Learn about radios, antennas, and electronics</li>
        <li>Use digital communication modes</li>
        <li>Learn and use Morse code</li>
        <li>Communicate through amateur radio satellites</li>
        <li>Operate portable stations from parks and outdoor locations</li>
        <li>Participate in contests and special events</li>

      <h2 id="getting-started">Getting started</h2>
      <p>Getting started is easier than you might think. The first step is learning the basics and preparing for your Amateur Radio license exam. You don’t need prior radio experience or expensive equipment to begin. Experienced ham radio operators have a long tradition of helping newcomers prepare for their license, choose equipment, learn good operating practices, and make their first contacts.</p>
