## 🛰️ Frequency Info

- **Frequency:** 146.700 MHz  
- **Offset:** -0.600 MHz   
- **PL Tone:** 107.2 Hz
- **Mode:** FM

## 📡 Usage

This is the primary VHF repeater operated by **W8FY**. It is used for:

- backup repeater and Wires-X
- Skywarn operations during severe weather
