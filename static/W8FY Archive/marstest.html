ARMED FORCES DAY CROSSBAND TEST

The operation can be semi-casual but, don't take too long.

Listen for the stations on their designated frequencies.   That is where they
will transmit. The operator will announce their listening frequency.  That is
where you will transmit.  Check your equipment frequency settings closely
and be sure you only transmit on an amateur radio frequency on which you are
licensed to operate.

Schedules and frequencies of participating military stations
are listed on the Army MARS web site,

http://www.usarmymars.org/home/announcements


You can operate from your station or the club.

The club station will be open at various times during this event.
Call W8FY on 146.850 to see if the station is open and time is available for you to operate.
