ARRL Membership Application form instructions

 

Print and complete the Membership Application.

Do not send application to ARRL

Send or deliver application and payment in full to the club.

We accept cash or check.

Make check payable to Van Wert Amateur Radio Club.

If you wish to charge your dues to a credit card, complete the charge
area on the application.  Send or deliver the application, along with the
club commission of $15 for new member or $2 for renewing member,
to the club.
