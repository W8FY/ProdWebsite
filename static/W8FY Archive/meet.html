Meetings

 

We meet on the first and third Saturday of each month

except when that day is New Year, Independence Day, or Christmas.

Committee meetings are at 7:30 PM.  Regular meetings are at 8:00 PM.

 

Meetings are conducted at

The Van Wert County Office of Homeland Security and Emergency Management Agency

1220 Lincoln Highway, Van Wert, Ohio.
