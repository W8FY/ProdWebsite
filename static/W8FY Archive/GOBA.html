GOBA Update

 

Just checking in to see how your plans for GOBA (June 20-27)  route coverage volunteers and repeater usage are progressing.

 

Our staff route review and repeater check is scheduled for the weekend of March 7-8.  Staff will meet in Bluffton the morning of the 7th and will drive the route, check repeaters and view camps--> Bluffton (Sun)-->Defiance (Mon, Tues)-->Optional 50 mile loop (Tues)-->Bowling Green (Wed, Thurs)-->Optional 110/50 mile loop (Thurs).  We will spend Saturday night in Bowling Green.
On Sunday, March 8th we will meet at Bowling Green High School and do the remaining route and camps-->Bowling Green-->Ottawa (Fri)-->Van Wert (Base camp and last Saturday)-->Bluffton (Sun).
We will be in three vehicles and will be testing repeaters under worst case scenario of an HT with a 5 watt battery pack operating into a rubber duck.  We operate strictly 2 meters.  We know many areas have really nice UHF repeaters but for our operating environment, 2 meters is our best choice with more repeaters and better coverage generally available.  Each vehicle will also have a 2 meter mobile with mag mount to check coverage between ambulances and staff vehicles when HTs won't reach a repeater.  If you have anyone available to listen and help with radio checks, that would be great and we would welcome a brief QSO with locals while we are testing.  Testing will mainly be between myself (N8SUZ) and Jim Hedrick (AB8PV) if you want to listen for our calls.

If you have any questions or concerns, feel free to email me at n8suz@yahoo.com
