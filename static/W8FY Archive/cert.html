Field Exercise

 

On Saturday, October 16th, 2010 we will run a CERT field exercise from 10:00 am until 2:00 pm at the Emergency Management Agency.  Registration is open at 9:45 am; lunch, snacks and hydration will be provided. Please bring your CERT backpacks for use in the exercise. If enough arrive for several strike teams we will be able to implement an active communications network. Please bring your hand held radio if available.
