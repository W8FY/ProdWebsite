Hot Air Festival

 

Lots of activities are planned for the Hot Air Festival located at the Van Wert County Fairgrounds September 6-8 in Van Wert.

The club will be setting up a HF station and allowing the Boy Scouts an opportunity to learn about amateur radio.

Activities like this require several operators
.

Contact our Activities Manager, Ralph Shields, WB8YIH, to join the list of operators.
