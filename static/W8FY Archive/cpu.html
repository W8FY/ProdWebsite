Donate your computer to the club.

 

 

With the ever changing computer world, and the club's need for computers, we continue to seek more up to date equipment for our operations..

Do you have a computer that you don't need?  Before hauling or shipping to a recycling center and paying a disposal fee, consider a donation to the club.

We can accept desktop and laptop, working or not, with or without an operating system. Minimum requirement, processor speed of 1 GHz or greater.

Printers and other peripheral devices will also be considered.

Contact Dave, KA8ZGE,  Steve, WA8WKF, or stop by at the next meeting and let us know what you have available.
