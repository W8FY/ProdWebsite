Security Test for your connection

 

There is a router flaw using Universal Plug n'Play (UPnP).

What this does is normal on your side of the router. This on the LAN side UPnP is normally enabled. This is what allows programs and other hardware. Example playstation, x-box and skype to work without you opening ports on your router.

 

What has been found is that UPnP has been enabled on the WAN side. This is the port is the one that connects to the internet.

If this is enabled, this is not good. A person with your IP address can quarry your router. Can find out what kind of router and exploit it. And gain access to your network.

 

Here is a link that will take you to Steve Gibson's Shield's UP web page so you can check your's.

Click here Steve Gibson

If you have this enabled try disabling it in your routers settings. Then recheck. If the port is still enable you may have to make contact with the manufacture to see if there is an upgrade for your router. If none is available and you are concern by this you may have to purchase a new one.
