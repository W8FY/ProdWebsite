US 30 Yard Sale

August 9 - 11

 

 

 

Operation will take place from the club station on August 11 from 1300 Z to 2300 Z.

Operation will be CW on 7.044, SSB on  7.244 and 14.244,
and the EchoLink node 315705 on 146.700.

To obtain a certificate, send your QSO information along with a 9 x 12 inch
self addressed, stamped envelope to:

Van Wert, OH 45891-0602
