Van Wert Amateur Radio Club

Biggie Bash


The Biggie Bash will be going the whole month of March! Have fun!

Purpose

To contact Amateur Radio Stations in, or provide contacts from the location of currently unincorporated towns that are now or have been listed on Township, County, State, or National Maps at any time. These unincorporated towns are the biggies!


Biggies

A Biggie Station must be loacated no more than 1/2 mile from the center of a Biggie at the time of the contact for a Biggie to count. You can work any number of stations in the same Biggie. You can work the same station from a Biggie on a different Band, Mode (no cross mode) or they have earned a different Appointment or you are in a different Biggie than previously or you are at a location 5 miles or more from any previous location where you have contacted that station in that Biggie.

You must provide a Log and Summary for Verification by the Biggies Bash Committee that includes the Date, Time (GMT), Call Sign, Band, Mode, County, State, Operator Name, Your Location, Points for the Contact, and the Name of the Biggie.

Biggie Points
To earn an Award, your Summary must use the Biggies Bash Points System.

Contact Points

Repeater Contact - Phone = 100 Points, Digital = 200 Points
Simplex Contact - Phone =200 Points, Digital = 400 Points


Bonus Points

Work Across County Line(s) - Add 50 Points per county line
Work Across State Line(s) - Add 100 Points per state line

Work a Biggie from a Biggie - Double the contact Points!
Work a Council person from their Biggie - Add 100 Points

Work a Mayor from their Biggie - Double the Contact and Bonus Points!
Work a Biggies-Pedition; Any Single or Multi-operator Station that will be on locatin in a Biggie for two hours or more and gives one week prior notice of operation to the Biggies Bash Committee (may be announced on Saturday morning net at 10:30 a.m. on 146.85 repeater); all contacts add - 100 Points!

Biggies Awards

*Council Person Appointment*
For providing 10 Contacts from a (1) Biggie

****Mayor Appointment****
For a Council Person providing 50 Contacts from a (1) Biggie

***Biggies Certificate***
For the Ham who earns 10,000 Points

***Big Biggies Certificate***
For the Ham who earns 100,000 Points

***Big Bad Biggies Certificate***
For the Super Ham who earns 1,000,000 Points


For a Contact to be valid, one of the Amateur Radio Stations in the QSO must be a Member in Good Standing, of the Van Wert Amateur Radio Club Inc., P.O. Box 602, Van Wert, Ohio 45891.


All decisions as to the Rules, Validity of Contacts, Accuracy of Biggie Locations, and Presentation of Awards will be the responsibility of the Biggies Bash Committee, of the Van Wert Amateur Radio Club Inc.
