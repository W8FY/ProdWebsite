Flu Clinc

 

 

No date for 2013. the Van Wert County Health Department will
sponsor a Flu Clinc at the Van Wert County Fairgrounds.  The clinc will open
at 8:30 AM and amateur radio operators have been requested to supply
support communications to the department.

Operations will be on two meter FM voice and operators should contact
our Activities Manager, Ralph Shields, WB8YIH for further information.
