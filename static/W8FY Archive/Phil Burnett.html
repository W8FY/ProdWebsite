I am also a member of the Radio Club--
I will gladly speak to the Club regarding Renewable Energies. I must warn you that once I open my mouth
it can be difficult to shut me up. This is a subject that I find fascinating and very important to each
and every citizen of this great nation. Energy is our Achilles heel. If allowed I would like to use some
photos and diagrams--either as hand outs, projections or display boards. I would like to cover nuclear
reactors as are current designs to include operation, advantages, disadvantages, liabilities and waste.
I would contrast this design with two other types of reactors that have been designed and prototypes in
operation for evaluation purposes. The other two reactor designs, one is refereed to as a PEBBLE BED
REACTOR and the other reactor design uses THORIUM and molten salts vs enriched uranium and high pressure steam.

To clear confusion I am not a nuclear designer or scientist. I worked at a nuclear weapons plant for
14 years where I was the Maintenance Tech Support for a Plutonium Recovery Building. This was Bldg 371
at the Rocky Flats Nuclear Weapons Plant located between Golden and Boulder Colorado. This building
contained 14.2 tons of PU238 (Plutonium) and was once refereed to as the most expensive per square foot
building on the face of the earth according to Ripples. I toured Fort Saint Verain Nuclear Power Plant
when it was being defueled, I've have also spent time at the Santa Suzanna facility where fuel rods are
decladed for further processing to recover nuclear materials to be reused. I have spent time at Savanna
River at the Glass Vitrification Facility where high level nuclear waste is processed for long term
storage. This high level waste was dried, mixed with sand put into a large stainless steel vessel then
heated to melt the sand and safely immobilize the nuclear waste for long term storage in a controlled
environment. I have also been involved with Natural Gas used as an alternate fuel for mass transit.
I worked in Saint Louis for 12 years operating and maintaining a high pressure, gas compression facility
for the Laclead Gas Company. This facility would compress natural gas from 120 PSI to 5000 PSI. Now I
am retired and tinker with wind generators and hot water solar panels. One day I hope to experiment with
biomass and produce methane to run a generator and also to heat water. As in my last email I am working
with Vantage Carree Center to bring to Van Wert an alternate energy fair. This I would also like to cover
so the members of the Armature Radio Club have a heads up and if so interested I would very much enjoy
anyone that would like to be involved.
