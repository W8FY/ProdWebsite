Each year the club shares a booth at the fair with the Van Wert County Emergency Management Agency.

Located in the Commercial Building, the booth provides a location for members to share amateur radio with the public.  There will be a two meter FM voice station and a HF digital mode station in the booth, in addition to various piecs of hand out literature.  We hope you will be available to spend some time in the booth and at the band show demonstrating and promoting amateur radio both as a hobby and a public service.

Contact Ralph Shields, our Activities Manager, for more information and to determine what time slots are available to you.
